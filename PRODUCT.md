# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS generated by `build.mjs` from `copy.mjs` (no framework). Hosted as static files from `dist/`.

## Users

- **Owners of a plot in Georgia** (Tbilisi–Mtskheta direction, Adjara: Batumi, Chakvi, Kobuleti). They want a garden house, guest room or seasonal cabin on land they already have. They compare a factory kit against local builders (KOHİ, Glimtrex, Nakaduri) and want the full price at their address, not only the kit price.
- **Hospitality operators**: guest houses, glamping sites, small hotels, wineries (Adjara, coastal Guria, Kakheti). They want one extra guest unit before the season and a batch afterwards.
- **Buyers of a ready home with land** (owner's own pilot: one plot in Adjara with a finished Naina 6×6 for sale; a second Elza as a showroom).
- Languages: Georgian is primary, Russian and English secondary. Mobile-first: traffic comes from Facebook/Instagram ads, ss.ge / myhome.ge style listings, WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Sell standard factory timber house kits made in Belarus (pine/spruce, kiln-dried planed timber, tongue-and-groove, wind lock; 44 mm main wall, 34 mm for small structures) and organise everything around them in Georgia: delivery, assembly, foundation, finish roofing, painting, utilities, insulation, furniture. Success = qualified lead with location + phone that turns into a signed estimate.

Line-up: 30+ standard models, 5–80 m²: garden houses, saunas, gazebos, terraces, sheds, outdoor toilets, canopies. Sizes of standard models are fixed by the factory and cannot be changed. Individual frame houses are a separate direction by individual brief.

## Positioning

"Choose the house — we organise everything else." A factory kit with a transparent, line-by-line price at your address: kit price public, assembly as a published percentage, everything else itemised. Competitors sell either a kit or a vague "turnkey".

## Confirmed facts (from the owner, 14.11.2026)

- Kit price from 12 000 GEL; most popular model ≈ 28 000 GEL (kept internal, not published per model).
- Kit includes: walls (planed kiln-dried timber, tongue-and-groove), wooden windows and doors with single-chamber glazing, fastener kit, rafter system and roof battens, floor (20 mm floor boards, joists).
- Not included: finish roof covering, delivery, assembly, foundation, painting, utilities, furniture.
- Lead time when not in stock: 1.5–2 months after prepayment.
- Assembly on a ready foundation incl. finish roof covering: 15–20% of kit price. Delivery not included.
- Self-pickup needs a side-loading truck or a crane truck (manipulator) with a body at least as long as the package (up to ~6 m).
- Showroom: Batumi, 13 Agmashenebeli St. — in preparation, opening date not announced.
- International delivery (Armenia, Turkey, others) possible, calculated individually — never claim it is routine.
- Never claim a service life; never claim winter-readiness flatly (use "models with additional insulation can be prepared for year-round use").
