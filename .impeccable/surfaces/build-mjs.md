# Surface brief: landing pages (index, hospitality, property)

Scope: three Persuade landings, ka (primary) / ru / en, generated by build.mjs from copy.mjs + site.config.mjs.
Audience: Georgian plot owners (B2C), hospitality operators (B2B), buyers of a finished home with land (owner's Adjara pilot).
Job: understand the full price at my address, trust the kit, leave phone + location.
Unresolved: real phone/WhatsApp, form endpoint, per-model prices, native Georgian proofreading, real photos.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a road atlas route from the Belarusian factory to the visitor's own plot; every section is a stage on that route. Refuses the category default of a forest-photo hero with "eco wooden houses" and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Road-atlas sheet. Cool map-paper ground with faint contour lines; legend palette with jobs: route red = action and route line, highway yellow = prices, territory green = included in the kit, grey hatch = not included / separate work, water blue = info links. FiraGO (Spiekermann wayfinding lineage, covers Mkhedruli, Cyrillic, Latin), heavy for place-name headlines, tabular figures for every number. Pins, km-post markers, scale bars, legend swatches as the component language.

STORY: I see my house type and the "from 12 000 GEL" price, I see exactly what arrives and what is separate, I see the route and timing to my village, I type my village and phone.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: H1 + working estimate form (village, model, phone CTA) + price legend. Right: large render framed as a map inset with scale bar. Full-width route strip under it: factory → Batumi warehouse → your plot, drawn on load.

FORM: Georgian road atlas / strip route map, list position 6. seed: d672f019. Raises: daylight-section (house drawn in section to show kit layers), split-flap (fixed-column rows with status states for models), sampler (strict top-to-bottom stage order).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
